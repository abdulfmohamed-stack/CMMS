KPI definitions and logic

PM on-time compliance
Scheduled PMs completed within their scheduled month ÷ PMs scheduled. Late completions count as missed. Target —.
Life-support PM compliance
The same measure restricted to life-support and high-risk equipment, held to — under Joint Commission EC.02.04.03.
Cost of service ratio (COSR)
Total service cost (labor, parts, contracts) over 12 months ÷ acquisition value of supported equipment. Commonly cited HTM range —.
Imaging uptime
Scheduled hours available ÷ scheduled hours for imaging modalities, averaged across sites. Target —.
Corrective turnaround
Hours from work-order open to completion, median per site, then averaged across sites.
Repeat failure
Corrective work orders on an asset within 30 days of a prior corrective close ÷ corrective work orders closed.
Replacement risk score (0–100)
Age vs. expected life (35), condition (24), end-of-support (15), failures in 12 months (15), utilization (10). Plan year: 75+ next year, then one year later per 10 points, down to 35.
How this runs in Power BI
A star schema over CMMS extracts (FactWorkOrder, FactPM, FactServiceCost, DimAsset, DimSite, DimDate), each KPI a documented DAX measure, refreshed on a scheduled cadence with data-quality rules run in Power Query before load.
